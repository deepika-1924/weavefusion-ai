# WEAVEFUSION AI — AI-GENERATED SUSTAINABLE FASHION FROM INDIAN HANDLOOMS

WeaveFusion AI is a generative design sandbox that fuses India's 104 GI-tagged handloom traditions with Western silhouettes on demand, turning static heritage fabric into a live, wearable, sustainable fashion concept.

**Challenges:** July Creative Industries Challenge

**GitHub:** https://github.com/deepika-1924/weavefusion-ai

**Live Demo:** https://weavefusion-ai.vercel.app

---

### The Issue

Fast fashion drives overproduction and environmental damage, while India's handloom clusters carriers of centuries of textile heritage struggle for visibility with modern, style-conscious buyers. There's no way for someone to see what a sustainable handloom fabric would actually look like as a silhouette they'd wear today, and building a static lookbook of every handloom-by-style combination isn't feasible for a small team.

### Our Magic Solution

Instead of pre-rendering a fixed image library, WeaveFusion AI generates each outfit live. A user picks a handloom (e.g. Pochampally Ikat) and a Western silhouette (e.g. Streetwear); our orchestration layer runs two branches in parallel watsonx.ai's Granite model pulls the fabric's heritage story and a sustainability scorecard, while Replicate's image models render a complete hybrid outfit from a fused prompt. The two outputs merge into one live dashboard render. This turns a static content problem into an infinitely scalable generative one the same architecture that handles the pilot handlooms today extends to all 104 GI-registered clusters without rebuilding anything.
